Breaking a Lease in Oklahoma: What 41 O.S. §§111, 115 and 129 Say

Last checked: 2026-11-17. Each cited section re-read that day on OSCN or the linked source; statute pack weekly check 2026-11-16 found no change · informational, not legal advice

Informational, not legal advice. This page summarizes what the statute says in plain English. It doesn't tell you how a court would rule on your facts. Talk to a licensed Oklahoma attorney before you act.

Quick answer

Short answer: 41 O.S. §111 gives no general right to end a fixed-term lease early just by giving notice. Unless ended earlier under the Act or otherwise agreed, a fixed-term lease "expires on the ending date thereof without notice" (41 O.S. §111(C)).

A lease can still end early in specific ways: a domestic violence, sexual violence or stalking victim's written notice plus a protective order (§111(F)); a servicemember's written notice plus military orders under the federal SCRA (50 U.S.C. §3955); certain landlord breaches (§§121, 123); or a written agreement between landlord and tenant. If a tenant simply moves out early, §129(B) requires the landlord to make reasonable efforts to re-rent, and that limits what the tenant owes.

What the law says

  • Fixed-term lease (§111(C)): "Unless earlier terminated under the provisions of the Oklahoma Residential Landlord and Tenant Act or unless otherwise agreed upon, a tenancy for a definite term expires on the ending date thereof without notice." 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Month-to-month or at will (§111(A)): either the landlord or the tenant may end it with written notice at least 30 days before the termination date. The 30 days run from the date the notice is served under §111(E). Tenancies shorter than month-to-month (for example week-to-week) take 7 days' written notice, served under §111(E) (§111(B)). 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • How a notice is served (§111(E)): a notice to the tenant goes personally to the tenant; if the tenant can't be located, to a family member over 12 who lives there; otherwise it is posted in a conspicuous place AND mailed by certified mail or the USPS Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail. A notice to the landlord goes to the landlord personally, or if that can't be done, by certified mail. "Landlord" here means anyone authorized to receive notices under §116. 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Domestic violence, sexual violence or stalking (§111(F)): "A victim of domestic violence, sexual violence or stalking may terminate a lease without penalty by providing written notice and a protective order of an incident of such violence within thirty (30) days of such incident, unless the landlord waives such time period." Both pieces are required: written notice and a protective order, within 30 days of the incident unless the landlord waives the time period. 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • No retaliation against victims (§113.3): a landlord "shall not deny, refuse to renew or terminate a tenancy" because an applicant, tenant or household member is a victim or alleged victim of domestic violence, sexual violence or stalking, "regardless of whether there exists a current protective order," and "shall not deny a tenancy or retaliate against a tenant because the applicant or tenant has previously terminated a rental agreement" for that reason. 41 O.S. §113.3 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Tenant just moves out early (§129(B)): if the tenant "wrongfully quits and abandons" during the term, the landlord "shall make reasonable efforts to make the dwelling unit available for rental." If the landlord re-rents for a term starting before the old lease ends, the old lease ends when the new tenancy starts. If the landlord fails to use reasonable efforts, or accepts the abandonment as a surrender, the lease is treated as ended by the landlord as of the date the landlord has notice of the abandonment. If the landlord makes reasonable efforts but can't re-rent "for a fair rental during the term," the tenant is liable for "the entire rent or the difference in rental, whichever may be appropriate, for the remainder of the term." For month-to-month or week-to-week tenancies, the "term" for this purpose is a month or a week. 41 O.S. §129 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Duty to mitigate (§105(A)): "An aggrieved party under the provisions of this act has a duty to mitigate damages." In an action for breach of a rental agreement or to enforce the Act, "the prevailing party shall be entitled to reasonable attorneys' fees" (§105(B)). 41 O.S. §105 · last checked 2026-11-17

Military orders: the federal SCRA

41 O.S. §111, the Act's termination section, contains no military or servicemember provision (re-read 2026-11-17). The lease-termination right for servicemembers comes from federal law, the Servicemembers Civil Relief Act. 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-17

  • Which leases (§3955(b)(1)): a lease of premises occupied or intended to be occupied by a servicemember or dependents, if (A) the lease was signed before the person entered military service; (B) the servicemember, while in military service, signed the lease and later received orders for a permanent change of station or to deploy "for a period of not less than 90 days"; or (C) the servicemember signed on such orders and then received a qualifying stop movement order. 50 U.S.C. §3955 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • How (§3955(c)): the tenant delivers written notice of termination and a copy of the servicemember's military orders to the landlord or the landlord's agent. Delivery may be by hand, by private business carrier, by U.S. mail with sufficient postage and return receipt requested, or by the electronic means listed in §3955(c)(2)(D). "Military orders" include official orders (including separation or retirement) or a notification, certification or verification from the commanding officer (§3955(i)(1)). 50 U.S.C. §3955 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • When it ends (§3955(d)): for a lease covered by §3955(b)(1)(A) or (B) with monthly rent, termination "is effective 30 days after the first date on which the next rental payment is due and payable after the date on which the notice ... is delivered." Other leases under (b)(1)(A) or (B) end "on the last day of the month following the month in which the notice is delivered"; a stop-movement lease under (b)(1)(C) ends on the date the notice and orders are delivered (§3955(d)(1)(B)). 50 U.S.C. §3955 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Money (§3955(e)(1), (f)): rent for the period before termination is prorated. "The lessor may not impose an early termination charge," but taxes, summonses and other obligations under the lease, "including reasonable charges to the lessee for excess wear," that are due and unpaid stay payable. Rent paid in advance for after the termination date must be refunded within 30 days of the effective date. 50 U.S.C. §3955 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Also in §3955: a spouse or dependent may terminate within one year after a servicemember dies in service (§3955(a)(3)); a landlord may apply to a court before the termination date for relief "as justice and equity require" (§3955(g)); and knowingly holding a servicemember's belongings or security deposit to cover rent accruing after a lawful termination is a federal misdemeanor (§3955(h)). 50 U.S.C. §3955 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Oklahoma state military forces on state active duty or Title 32 duty: 44 O.S. §208.1 adopts the SCRA's civil protections as state law for members of the state military forces ordered to state active duty or Title 32 active duty, "except where state law may provide additional or superior protections." 44 O.S. §208.1 · last checked 2026-11-17

When the landlord's breach ends the lease

  • Material breach of the lease, or a §118 breach that materially affects health or safety (§121(A)): the tenant may deliver written notice specifying the breach and that the lease will terminate on a date not less than 30 days after receipt if the breach isn't remedied within 14 days. 41 O.S. §121 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Essential services (§121(C)) and uninhabitable units (§121(D)): if the landlord willfully or negligently fails to supply heat, running water, hot water, electric, gas or another essential service, the tenant may, after written notice, immediately terminate (among other remedies). If a noncompliance makes the unit uninhabitable or poses an imminent threat to health and safety and isn't remedied as promptly as conditions require, the tenant may immediately terminate on written notice specifying the noncompliance. None of these rights arise until the tenant has given written notice, or if the tenant (or household, pets or guests) caused the condition (§121(E)). 41 O.S. §121 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Lockout (§123): if a landlord wrongfully removes or excludes a tenant, the tenant may recover possession in court or terminate after giving notice, and in either case recover up to twice the average monthly rent or twice actual damages, whichever is greater. If the lease is terminated, the landlord "shall return all deposits recoverable" under §115 "and all prepaid and unearned rent." 41 O.S. §123 · last checked 2026-11-17

The security deposit when a lease ends early

  • What it can cover (§115(B)): on termination, the deposit "may be applied to the payment of accrued rent and the amount of damages which the landlord has suffered by reason of the tenant's noncompliance" with the Act and the rental agreement. 41 O.S. §115 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Itemize and deliver (§115(B)): deductions must be "itemized by the landlord in a written statement delivered by mail to be by return receipt requested and to be signed for by any person of statutory service age at such address or in person to the tenant if he can reasonably be found." 41 O.S. §115 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Deadline (§115(B)): return the balance, without interest, within 45 days after termination of the tenancy, delivery of possession and the tenant's written demand. If the tenant makes no written demand within 6 months after termination, the deposit reverts to the landlord. 41 O.S. §115 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • Not last month's rent (§115(F)): "Except as otherwise provided by the rental agreement," a tenant may not apply the deposit to the last month's rent or use it instead of rent. 41 O.S. §115 · last checked 2026-11-17
  • If the landlord doesn't comply (§115(E)): the tenant may recover the deposit and any prepaid rent. §115(G) keeps other damages under the Act available to either side. 41 O.S. §115 · last checked 2026-11-17

How experienced landlords do it

Practice notes come from John Ogorzalek, 5 Dogs Properties (owned 300+ units since 2014). They are habits, not substitutes for the statute.

Practice (not law): When a tenant moves out, send a written list of rent-ready expenses (cleaning, repairs beyond wear, rekey) tied to the move-in/move-out checklist, along with the §115 itemized statement. The statute's delivery rule still applies: return-receipt mail or in person.

Practice (not law): When you serve a notice, hand it only to an adult 18 or older. If no adult is there, post it AND mail it. (§111(E) itself allows a household family member over 12.)

FAQ

Can a tenant legally break a lease in Oklahoma?

Only in specific situations. A fixed-term lease expires on its end date unless ended earlier under the Act or otherwise agreed (41 O.S. §111(C)). Early-termination routes include a domestic violence victim's written notice and protective order (41 O.S. §111(F)), military orders under 50 U.S.C. §3955, certain landlord breaches (41 O.S. §121, 41 O.S. §123), or a written agreement.

How does a domestic violence victim end a lease in Oklahoma?

Under 41 O.S. §111(F), by providing written notice and a protective order of an incident within 30 days of the incident, unless the landlord waives the time period. The statute says the lease ends without penalty.

What does a tenant owe after moving out early?

Under 41 O.S. §129(B), the landlord must make reasonable efforts to re-rent. If the landlord re-rents, the old lease ends when the new tenancy starts. If reasonable efforts fail to re-rent for a fair rental, the tenant is liable for the entire rent or the difference in rental for the rest of the term.

Can a servicemember break a lease in Oklahoma?

Yes, under federal law when the lease qualifies under 50 U.S.C. §3955(b)(1): written notice plus a copy of the military orders. For most leases with monthly rent, termination takes effect 30 days after the next rent due date after the notice is delivered (stop-movement and non-monthly leases follow different timing, §3955(d)), and the landlord may not impose an early termination charge.

How much notice ends a month-to-month lease in Oklahoma?

At least 30 days' written notice, running from the date of service under 41 O.S. §111(E) (41 O.S. §111(A)). See the 30-day notice page.
