Did Oklahoma landlord-tenant law change in 2026?

Short answer: No. No bill from Oklahoma’s 2026 session changed the Residential Landlord and Tenant Act or the eviction statutes. Several were proposed. None became law.

Residential Landlord and Tenant Act (41 O.S.) and forcible entry and detainer provisions (12 O.S.) · Oklahoma Legislature subject index, Landlord and Tenant, 2026 session · last checked 2026-10-26 · informational, not legal advice

Where the “2026 Landlord and Tenant Act” idea came from

Some online posts describe an “Oklahoma Landlord and Tenant Act of 2026.” That is the title of HB3681, a shell bill: its official title only creates the act, provides for noncodification and provides an effective date. It has no substantive provisions. Shell bills are placeholders lawmakers can fill in later; this one never was. It was referred to House Rules on 02/03/2026, saw no further action, and died when the session ended. HB3681 bill history · last checked 2026-10-26

The underlying act has seen little change for decades: Oklahoma Watch reported that it “has barely changed since” 1978. Oklahoma Watch, May 20, 2026

Bills owners heard about: final status

HB3681 · “Landlord and Tenant Act of 2026”

Proposed: shell only (title, noncodification, effective date).

Status: dead. Referred to House Rules 02/03/2026; no further action.

HB2015 · tenant remedies

Proposed: changes to tenant remedies, damages and rent withholding.

Status: dead. Passed the House 52-39 on 03/26/2026. Engrossed to the Senate 03/30/2026, referred to Senate Judiciary 04/01/2026, never heard. It cannot carry over: 2026 was the 2nd and final session of the 60th Legislature, so a 2027 version would need a new bill in the 61st Legislature.

SB1209 · eviction timeline

Proposed: exclude Sundays and holidays from the eviction (FED) trial period.

Status: dead. Senate: failed 19-21 on 03/11/2026, then passed 26-18 on 03/16/2026. House: failed 35-53 on 05/06/2026; reconsidered; failed again 40-51 on 05/07/2026. Its 2025 predecessor, SB128, was vetoed on 05/05/2025.

HB3386 · eviction mediation

Proposed: mediation in evictions where minor children live in the unit.

Status: dead. Cleared the House only; never got a Senate floor vote. Some trackers label it “passed” or effective Nov. 1, 2026. It is not law.

SB1769 · short-term rental guest ID

Proposed: let STR owners require guest photo ID.

Status: dead. Cleared the Senate only; never got a House floor vote. Some trackers label it “passed” or effective Nov. 1, 2026. It is not law.

FAQ

Did Oklahoma pass a new landlord-tenant act in 2026?

No. No bill from Oklahoma's 2026 session changed the Residential Landlord and Tenant Act or the eviction statutes. Several were proposed. None became law (last checked 2026-10-26).

Is the "Oklahoma Landlord and Tenant Act of 2026" a real law?

No. That is the title of HB3681, a shell bill containing only a title, a noncodification clause and an effective date. It was referred to House Rules on 02/03/2026, saw no further action, and died when the session ended.

What happened to HB 2015?

HB2015 (tenant remedies) passed the House 52-39 on 03/26/2026. It was engrossed to the Senate 03/30/2026 and referred to Senate Judiciary 04/01/2026, where it died without a hearing. 2026 was the second and final session of the 60th Legislature, so it cannot carry over; any 2027 version would need a new bill in the 61st Legislature.

Did the eviction timeline change in 2026?

No. SB1209, which would have excluded Sundays and holidays from the eviction (FED) trial period, passed the Senate 26-18 on 03/16/2026 but failed in the House 35-53 on 05/06/2026 and, after reconsideration, failed again 40-51 on 05/07/2026. No other 2026 bill amending the eviction statutes became law.

Do I need a new lease or new notices because of 2026 changes?

No 2026 law change requires it, because nothing enacted in 2026 changed the Residential Landlord and Tenant Act or the eviction statutes.
