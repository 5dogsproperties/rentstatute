Oklahoma Lease Violation Notice: The 10-Day Remedy and 15-Day Termination Rules (41 O.S. §132)

41 O.S. §132 last re-read on OSCN 2026-10-19; statute pack weekly check 2026-11-02 found no change · informational, not legal advice

Informational, not legal advice. This page summarizes what the statute says in plain English. It doesn't tell you how a court would rule on your facts. Talk to a licensed Oklahoma attorney before you act.

Quick answer

Short answer: For a material non-rent lease violation, 41 O.S. §132(B) lets an Oklahoma landlord serve written notice that the lease ends at least 15 days after the tenant receives it unless the tenant remedies the breach within 10 days. If the tenant remedies, any later breach allows immediate termination on written notice.

If the problem can be fixed by repair, replacement, or cleaning, §132(A) gives a second path: 10 days to fix it, then the landlord may do the work and bill the cost as rent. Unpaid rent uses a different notice (§131, 5 days), and some serious conduct allows immediate action under §132(C)–(D).

What the law says (§132)

  • (A) Fixable by repair, replacement, or cleaning: serve written notice as §111(E) requires. If the tenant doesn't fix it within 10 days (or promptly in an emergency), the landlord may do the work and bill the cost as rent on the next due date. If the landlord does the fix this way, the landlord can't also terminate for that breach. 41 O.S. §132 · last checked 2026-10-19
  • (B) Material noncompliance: serve written notice that the lease ends at least 15 days after receipt unless the tenant remedies within 10 days. Any later breach allows immediate termination on written notice. 41 O.S. §132 · last checked 2026-10-19
  • (C) Imminent, irremediable harm to the premises or a person, not promptly remedied: the landlord may terminate by immediately filing a forcible entry and detainer (eviction) case. 41 O.S. §132 · last checked 2026-10-19
  • (D) Criminal or drug activity: criminal activity that threatens others' health, safety, or peaceful enjoyment, or drug-related criminal activity on or near the premises, by the tenant, household, or guests, is grounds for immediate termination. 41 O.S. §132 · last checked 2026-10-19

Tenant duties a violation often involves: keep the unit clean and safe, dispose of waste, use facilities reasonably, don't damage the unit or disturb neighbors, and no drug-related criminal activity on or near the premises by the tenant, household, or guests. 41 O.S. §127 · last checked 2026-10-14

How to serve the notice (§111(E))

§132(A) and §132(B) notices are both served as §111(E) lists, in this order: 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-10-26

  1. Deliver it to the tenant personally.
  2. If the tenant can't be found, deliver it to a family member over 12 who lives there.
  3. Otherwise, post it in a conspicuous place AND mail a copy by certified mail or USPS Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail.

The §132(B) 15 days run from the tenant's receipt, so a notice that is posted and mailed may be received later than the day you post it. Leave a buffer, and ask counsel how receipt is counted in your case.

Which notice fits?

  • Unpaid rent: use the §131 written demand (termination if rent isn't paid within 5 days). See the 5-day late-rent notice. 41 O.S. §131 · last checked 2026-10-14
  • Ending a month-to-month tenancy without a violation: 30 days' written notice under §111. See the 30-day notice.
  • Pet the tenant says is an assistance animal: stop and review 41 O.S. §113.2 and the federal Fair Housing Act with counsel first. A knowingly false assistance-animal claim can be handled under §132, but federal FHA/HUD guidance still controls and there is no blanket denial.
  • Tenant raises domestic violence, sexual violence, or stalking: §111(F) lets the victim end the lease without penalty with written notice plus a protective order within 30 days of the incident (unless the landlord waives the time period). Talk to counsel.
  • After termination: if the tenant stays, possession goes through the court process. See the eviction process. Never lock out or remove belongings: a wrongful removal or exclusion triggers 41 O.S. §123 (up to the greater of 2× average monthly rent or 2× actual damages).

Practice (not law)

Practice (not law): For an unauthorized pet, offer a signed pet addendum (pet fee, pet deposit, rules) or removal within 10 days, using the §132(B) notice. Assistance animals are different: no fees under federal FHA guidance.

Practice (not law): Hand notices only to an adult 18 or older. If no adult is present, post AND mail. This is stricter than the statute, which allows a household family member over 12 (41 O.S. §111(E)).

Checklist before you send

  1. Confirm the ground: rent (use §131), fixable damage (§132(A)), material noncompliance (§132(B)), or imminent harm / criminal activity (§132(C)–(D), talk to counsel).
  2. Quote the lease clause or the §127 duty and describe what happened, with dates.
  3. State what the tenant must do to remedy and the 10-day deadline.
  4. For §132(B), set a termination date at least 15 days after the tenant receives the notice. Leave a buffer if posting and mailing.
  5. Serve under §111(E) and keep proof of service. §132(A) and §132(B) both require §111(E) service.
  6. Check for an assistance-animal request (§113.2, FHA) or a domestic-violence situation (§111(F)) before sending.
  7. Calendar day 10 and the termination date. If the tenant remedies, note that a later breach allows immediate termination on written notice.

FAQ

How much notice does an Oklahoma landlord give for a lease violation?

For material noncompliance, 41 O.S. §132(B) uses written notice that the lease ends at least 15 days after the tenant receives it unless the tenant remedies within 10 days.

Can the tenant fix the violation and stay?

Yes. If the tenant remedies within 10 days, the lease doesn't end for that breach under 41 O.S. §132(B). Any later breach allows immediate termination on written notice.

Can I fix tenant damage myself and charge the tenant?

If the breach can be fixed by repair, replacement, or cleaning and the tenant doesn't fix it within 10 days after written notice, 41 O.S. §132(A) lets the landlord do the work and bill the cost as rent on the next due date. If you do that, you can't also terminate for the same breach.

Can an Oklahoma landlord terminate immediately for drug activity?

41 O.S. §132(D) makes criminal activity that threatens others' health, safety, or peaceful enjoyment, or drug-related criminal activity on or near the premises by the tenant, household, or guests, grounds for immediate termination. Talk to counsel before acting.

Is a lease violation notice the same as a 5-day notice?

No. Unpaid rent uses the 41 O.S. §131 written demand (5 days). Non-rent violations use 41 O.S. §132.
