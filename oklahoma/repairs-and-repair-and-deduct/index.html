Oklahoma Repair-and-Deduct Law: Landlord Repair Duties and Tenant Remedies (41 O.S. §§118, 121)

41 O.S. §121 re-read on OSCN 2026-11-02; §118 re-read on OSCN 2026-11-03 (unchanged since 2026-10-14 pack); statute pack weekly check 2026-11-02 found no change · informational, not legal advice

Informational, not legal advice. This page summarizes what the statute says in plain English. It doesn't tell you how a court would rule on your facts. Talk to a licensed Oklahoma attorney before you act.

Quick answer

Short answer: Oklahoma's repair-and-deduct rule (41 O.S. §121(B)) starts with the tenant's written notice. The landlord then has 14 days to fix it (or as promptly as an emergency requires). Only after that may the tenant do the work, give the landlord an itemized statement, and deduct the cost from rent, capped at one month's rent, and only for a breach that materially affects health.

The landlord's repair duties are in 41 O.S. §118: keep the unit fit and habitable and keep supplied systems and appliances, including AC, in safe working order. None of the §121 tenant remedies apply until the tenant gives written notice, or if the tenant (or their family, pets, or guests) caused the problem.

What the law says: landlord repair duties (§118)

  • Make repairs and do what's needed to keep the unit fit and habitable. 41 O.S. §118 · last checked 2026-11-03
  • Keep supplied electrical, plumbing, sanitary, heating, ventilation, air conditioning, and appliances in safe working order. 41 O.S. §118 · last checked 2026-11-03
  • Keep common areas clean and safe (not required for single-family homes). 41 O.S. §118 · last checked 2026-11-03
  • Provide trash receptacles and removal (not required for 1–2 family homes or where the government provides it). 41 O.S. §118 · last checked 2026-11-03
  • (A)(5): except for single-family homes or units with independently metered utilities, supply running water, reasonable hot water at all times, and reasonable heat. 41 O.S. §118 · last checked 2026-11-03
  • (B): landlord and tenant may agree that the tenant does specified repairs or maintenance, but only in a conspicuous writing separate from the lease. 41 O.S. §118 · last checked 2026-11-03
  • (C): before the lease starts, disclose known or suspected methamphetamine manufacture in the unit or premises (no disclosure needed if tested contamination is at or below 0.1 µg/100 cm²). 41 O.S. §118 · last checked 2026-11-03

What the law says: tenant remedies (§121)

  • (A) Material breach affecting health or safety: the tenant gives written notice. If it isn't fixed within 14 days, the lease ends on a date at least 30 days after the landlord receives the notice. 41 O.S. §121 · last checked 2026-11-02
  • (B) Repair-and-deduct: for a breach that materially affects health where the repair costs equal to or less than one month's rent: written notice from the tenant, then 14 days for the landlord to fix it (or as promptly as an emergency requires), then the tenant may do the work, give the landlord an itemized statement, and deduct the cost from rent. The 2022 amendment (Laws 2022, c. 230) replaced the old $100 limit with the one-month-rent cap. 41 O.S. §121 · last checked 2026-11-02
  • (C) Essential services (heat, running or hot water, electric, gas) willfully or negligently not supplied: after written notice, the tenant may end the lease immediately, procure the service and deduct the cost, recover damages for reduced rental value, or get substitute housing and be excused from rent for that period. 41 O.S. §121 · last checked 2026-11-02
  • (D) Uninhabitable or imminent threat not fixed promptly: the tenant may end the lease immediately with written notice. 41 O.S. §121 · last checked 2026-11-02
  • (E) none of these rights arise until the tenant gives written notice, or if the tenant, their family, pets, or guests caused the problem. 41 O.S. §121 · last checked 2026-11-02

2026 bills: HB2015 (2026), which would have rewritten the §121 tenant remedies, did not become law. See 2026 Oklahoma law changes.

Related rules: entry, shutoffs, tenant-caused damage

  • Getting in to fix it: outside an emergency, give at least 1 day's notice and enter at reasonable times; the tenant can't unreasonably refuse entry for repairs. See Oklahoma landlord entry rules. 41 O.S. §128 · last checked 2026-10-26
  • No shutoffs or lockouts: willfully or negligently cutting heat, water, electric, or gas triggers the §121(C) remedies above. A lockout is wrongful exclusion under 41 O.S. §123 (up to the greater of 2× average monthly rent or 2× actual damages). 41 O.S. §123 · last checked 2026-10-26
  • Tenant-caused damage: the §121 remedies don't apply. If the breach can be fixed by repair, replacement, or cleaning, the landlord may serve written notice under §132(A) and, if it isn't fixed in 10 days, do the work and bill it as rent. See Oklahoma lease violation notice. 41 O.S. §132 · last checked 2026-10-19

What this page doesn't cover

  • Rent withholding: this page doesn't cover withholding rent outside the §121 remedies. If rent goes unpaid, see the 5-day late-rent notice and talk to counsel before acting.

Practice (not law)

Practice (not law): Require written repair requests (portal or email) in the lease. Respond the same day with a timeline. Treat summer AC outages as urgent. The statute baseline is the §121(B) 14-day cure after written notice and the §118 duty to keep supplied AC in safe working order.

Practice notes come from John Ogorzalek, 5 Dogs Properties (owned 300+ units since 2014). They are habits, not substitutes for the statute.

Checklist when a repair request comes in

  1. Log the date and time you received the tenant's written notice. The §121 clocks run from written notice.
  2. Decide whether it affects health or safety, is an essential service (heat, water, hot water, electric, gas), or makes the unit uninhabitable. Those need fast action.
  3. Give entry notice (at least 1 day, unless an emergency) and fix it within the 14 days (or as promptly as an emergency requires).
  4. If the tenant caused it, document it with dated photos and consider a §132(A) notice instead.
  5. If a tenant deducts a repair from rent, compare it with the §121(B) steps (written notice, 14 days, itemized statement, one-month-rent cap) and talk to counsel before treating rent as unpaid.

FAQ

Is repair and deduct legal in Oklahoma?

Yes, within limits. Under 41 O.S. §121 subsection (B), for a breach that materially affects health, the tenant gives written notice. If the landlord doesn't fix it within 14 days (or as promptly as an emergency requires), the tenant may do the work, give an itemized statement, and deduct the cost from rent, capped at one month's rent.

How long does a landlord have to fix something in Oklahoma?

For a material breach affecting health or safety, the tenant's written notice starts a 14-day period. If it isn't fixed, the lease can end on a date at least 30 days after the landlord receives the notice (41 O.S. §121(A)). Repair-and-deduct also uses a 14-day period, or faster in an emergency (41 O.S. §121(B)).

Is the repair-and-deduct cap still $100 in Oklahoma?

No. The 2022 amendment (Laws 2022, c. 230) set the cap at one month's rent (41 O.S. §121).

Does an Oklahoma landlord have to fix the AC?

If you supply AC, 41 O.S. §118 requires you to keep it in safe working order. Whether a landlord must supply AC in the first place isn't covered here; ask counsel.

Can my tenant withhold rent for repairs?

The Act gives tenants specific remedies after written notice, including repair-and-deduct up to one month's rent (41 O.S. §121). Plain rent withholding isn't covered here; talk to counsel.
