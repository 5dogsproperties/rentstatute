Quick answer

Short answer: Under 41 O.S. §111(E), the written notice "required by the Oklahoma Residential Landlord and Tenant Act, to terminate any tenancy shall be served on the tenant or landlord personally unless otherwise specified by law." If the tenant cannot be located, service is made on a family member over twelve who resides with the tenant. If neither can be served, the notice is posted at a conspicuous place on the dwelling unit and a copy is mailed by certified mail or through the Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail. 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-24

What the law says: §111(E), step by step

  1. Personal service. The notice "shall be served on the tenant or landlord personally unless otherwise specified by law." 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-24
  2. Family member. "If the tenant cannot be located, service shall be made by delivering the notice to any family member of such tenant over the age of twelve (12) years residing with the tenant." 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-24
  3. Post and mail. "If service cannot be made on the tenant personally or on such family member, notice shall be posted at a conspicuous place on the dwelling unit of the tenant. If the notice is posted, a copy of such notice shall be mailed to the tenant by certified mail or by mailing such notice through the Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail as provided by the United States Post Office." 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-24
  4. Serving the landlord. "If service cannot be made on the landlord personally, the notice shall be mailed to the landlord by certified mail." For this subsection, "landlord" means "any person authorized to receive service of process and notice pursuant to Section 116 of this title." 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-24

Subsection E lists these methods. It doesn't list email or text message.

Which notices point to §111(E)

  • 30-day notice, month-to-month or tenancy at will (§111(A)). Written notice at least thirty days before the termination date; "the thirty-day period to terminate shall begin to run from the date notice to terminate is served as provided in subsection E." 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-24 See 30-day notice.
  • Less than month-to-month (§111(B)). Written notice "served as provided in subsection E of this section at least seven (7) days before the date upon which the termination is to become effective." 41 O.S. §111 · last checked 2026-11-24
  • Tenant's noncompliance (§132(A) and (B)). Both call for written notice "served as provided in subsection E of Section 111 of this title." Under (A), for a noncompliance "which noncompliance can be remedied by repair, replacement of a damaged item, or cleaning," the notice specifies the breach and requires the tenant to remedy it within ten days. Under (B), for a "material noncompliance," the notice specifies the acts and omissions constituting the noncompliance and says the rental agreement will terminate upon a date not less than fifteen days after receipt unless remedied within ten days. 41 O.S. §132 · last checked 2026-11-24 See lease-violation notice.
  • Unpaid rent (§131(B)). The landlord may terminate if the tenant fails to pay "within five (5) days after written notice of landlord's demand for payment." Section 131 itself doesn't state how that notice is delivered or refer to §111(E). Analysis: the statute pack treats serving it under §111(E) as common practice; confirm with counsel. 41 O.S. §131 · last checked 2026-11-24 See 5-day notice for late rent.
  • Holdover. See holdover tenants (§111(C), (D)).

Who accepts notices for the landlord: §116

  • "As a part of any rental agreement the lessor shall prominently and in writing identify what person at what address is entitled to accept service or notice under this act." 41 O.S. §116 · last checked 2026-11-24
  • The landlord, or a person authorized to enter into a rental agreement for the landlord, must disclose to the tenant in writing at or before the commencement of the tenancy the name and address of the person or persons authorized to manage the premises, the owner or owners, or a person authorized to act for the owner "for the purpose of receipt of service of process and receiving and receipting for notices." The information must be kept current. 41 O.S. §116 · last checked 2026-11-24
  • A person who fails to comply "becomes a landlord for the purposes of this act and an agent of each person who is otherwise a landlord for" receipt of service of process and "receiving and receipting for notices and demands," and for performing a landlord's obligations under the Act and the rental agreement. 41 O.S. §116 · last checked 2026-11-24

How experienced landlords do it

Practice notes come from John Ogorzalek, 5 Dogs Properties (owned 300+ units since 2014). They are habits, not substitutes for the statute.

Practice (not law): Hand notices only to an adult 18 or older. If no adult is present, post AND mail. (§111(E) itself allows a household family member over 12.)

Practice (not law): For a §131 rent demand, wait until rent is 5 days late before serving it, and put the due date and grace period in the lease. (§131 allows the demand once rent is unpaid when due.)

FAQ

How do you serve a termination notice on a tenant in Oklahoma?

Under 41 O.S. §111(E), the written notice is served on the tenant personally. If the tenant cannot be located, it is delivered to a family member over twelve residing with the tenant. If service cannot be made on either, it is posted at a conspicuous place on the dwelling unit and a copy is mailed by certified mail or through the Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail.

Can a landlord give a termination notice to the tenant's family member?

Under 41 O.S. §111(E), only "if the tenant cannot be located," and only to a family member of the tenant over the age of twelve residing with the tenant.

Is posting a termination notice on the door enough in Oklahoma?

No. 41 O.S. §111(E) says that if the notice is posted, "a copy of such notice shall be mailed to the tenant by certified mail or by mailing such notice through the Firm Mailing Book for Accountable Mail."

How is a termination notice served on a landlord in Oklahoma?

Under 41 O.S. §111(E), personally; if that cannot be done, by certified mail. "Landlord" there means a person authorized to receive service of process and notice under 41 O.S. §116.

Do the 5-day late-rent demand and the lease-violation notice have to be served the same way?

41 O.S. §132(A) and (B) require written notice "served as provided in subsection E of Section 111." 41 O.S. §131(B) calls for "written notice of landlord's demand for payment" and doesn't state a delivery method. The statute pack treats serving it under §111(E) as common practice; confirm with counsel.

Can a landlord serve a termination notice by email or text in Oklahoma?

41 O.S. §111(E) lists personal service, delivery to a family member over twelve, and posting plus certified or Firm Mailing Book mailing. It doesn't list email or text message. Ask counsel before relying on them.
